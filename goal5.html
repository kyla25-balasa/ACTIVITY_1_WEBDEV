kyla's Recipe for the Best Adobo

Ingredients

For the adobo

  • 1 kg chicken, cut into pieces
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • ½ cup vinegar
  • 1 cup water
  • 5 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 1 Onion
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 teaspoon whole peppercorns
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • Salt, to taste

Instructions

  1. Marinate the chicken in soy sauce and garlic for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Heat the cooking oil in a large pan and sauté the garlic until fragrant.
  3. Add the marinated chicken and cook until lightly browned.
  4. Pour in the soy sauce, vinegar, and water.
  5. Add the bay leaves and peppercorns.
  6. Bring to a boil. Do not stir immediately after adding the vinegar.
  7. Lower the heat, cover, and simmer for 30–40 minutes until the chicken is tender.
  8. Add sugar and adjust the taste with salt if needed.
  9. Continue cooking until the sauce becomes slightly thick and flavorful.
  10. Serve hot with steamed rice.
